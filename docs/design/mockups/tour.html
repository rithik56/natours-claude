About this tour

Ladakh is a high-altitude desert of monasteries, river valleys and mountain passes above 5,000 metres. This trip starts with two unhurried days in Leh, at around 3,500 metres, so your body can adjust before we go higher.

From Leh we cross Khardung La into the Nubra Valley, visit Diskit Monastery and the sand dunes and Bactrian camels at Hunder, then follow the Shyok river to the shore of Pangong Tso. We return over Chang La and finish at Hemis Monastery. Permits, a dedicated vehicle and driver, oxygen on board and an experienced local guide are included.
