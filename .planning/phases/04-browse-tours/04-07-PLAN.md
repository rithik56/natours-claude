## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal, quoted verbatim (not in user-story form; no story is invented): A traveller can find a tour that fits their fitness, dates and budget, and see its route, itinerary, guides, reviews and photo credits. API clients get the same catalogue.

<objective>
The streamed parts of the tour page (R4): the per-user booking panel with its four states (U11), the guides list and the paginated reviews (D-08), each behind a same-sized skeleton with its heading outside the boundary. The booking panel stops being a pinned bottom bar on phones (D-06, C1, ADR 0033).

Purpose: a traveller sees the future dates and price in the right place for a phone, knows when they are already booked, and reads guides and reviews, while the static content arrives first.
Output: `BookingSection`, `GuidesSection`, `ReviewsSection`, the bookings and reviews services, C1, ADR 0033.
</objective>

<decisions>
- **Booked means an upcoming paid booking** (QA-07, SEC-07). `findUpcomingPaid(userId, tourId, durationDays)` returns the earliest IST day of a `paid` booking whose `tripStatus` is upcoming, or null. plan.md 4.4 names `hasPaidBooking` returning a boolean; the panel needs the date, so the contract differs and ADR 0033 records it. Reversibility: reversible.
- **No pinned bottom bar** (D-06, C1) departs from plan.md 9.2, direction.md and the approved mockups; ADR 0033 records it (UI-SPEC Open item 2). Reversibility: reversible.
- **Review page parameter is `reviewPage`** (UI-SPEC Open item 12, named once here; integer at least 1, default 1, clamped). Reversibility: costly (shared URLs), settled now.
- **A review whose author populates as null is skipped and logged**, never thrown; a deactivated author's review stays visible (RESEARCH A9). Reversibility: reversible.
- **Unrated tours (QA-08) are not handled here**: every seed tour has four reviews; the default rating for a tour with none is Phase 6's decision (RESEARCH Open Question 7), noted for the Phase 6 plan.
</decisions>
