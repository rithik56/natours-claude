<decisions>
- **Stats are computed in memory from the cached catalogue** (pure `statsFrom`), so they need no separate cache entry and always agree with the list; ADR 0031's planned `['stats']` entry is therefore not used, and contracts.md says so. Reversibility: reversible.
- **The monthly plan reads every tour with an aggregation and is not cached** (its year comes from the URL). Reversibility: reversible.
- **Geo responses are not paginated**: `ok({ tours })` for within and `ok({ distances })` for distances. Reversibility: costly (API shape), settled before any client.
- **Distance cap 20,000** in either unit, so a nonsense radius is a 400 rather than a whole-planet query. Reversibility: reversible.
- **Roles match exactly** (`{ roles: ['guide', 'lead-guide', 'admin'] }`), per `src/server/access.ts`.
</decisions>
