## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal, quoted verbatim (not in user-story form; no story is invented): A traveller can find a tour that fits their fitness, dates and budget, and see its route, itinerary, guides, reviews and photo credits. API clients get the same catalogue.

<objective>
The filter, sort and paging experience on `/` (U10) on top of plan 04-04's query module: the D-01 disclosure, the five approved selects, the result count, the empty state with "Clear filters", text-only pagination above 12 tours, the first-row preload (C2), `formatMonth` (C5) and the page's metadata. Server-rendered only, with no client JavaScript (D-05).

Purpose: the core value is a traveller on a phone choosing a tour by fitness, dates and budget; this is that screen.
Output: `tour-filters`, `tour-list-url.ts`, `formatMonth`, the finished `/`, and its JavaScript-off Playwright spec.
</objective>
