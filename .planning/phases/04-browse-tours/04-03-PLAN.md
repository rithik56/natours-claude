## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal, quoted verbatim (not in user-story form; no story is invented): A traveller can find a tour that fits their fitness, dates and budget, and see its route, itinerary, guides, reviews and photo credits. API clients get the same catalogue.

<objective>
Finish D10: `--today` rolls only the start dates that have passed (ADR 0023), the Golden Triangle Friday rule applies, the seed checks the `seed/README.md` rules afterwards, and the seed is wired into CI and into the Playwright app project with the clock pinned to 8 Nov 2026.

Purpose: every tour always has a bookable departure however late the seed runs, past trips stay past, and every later page test runs on known data and a known date.
Output: `scripts/seed/dates.ts`, `scripts/seed/rules.ts`, the CI seed step, the Playwright seed project, ADR 0030.
</objective>

<decisions>
- **"Passed" means the IST day is on or before today** (RESEARCH A6), so a departure on today's date (unbookable) moves and every tour keeps a bookable date. Reversibility: reversible.
- **Playwright seeds with `--reset --today=2026-11-08` against the dev database (`natours`).** Re-running resets seed data, which is reproducible; documented in the README. Reversibility: reversible.
- **CI seeds with the real date** (no `--today`), because smoke and Lighthouse run `next start` without APP_NOW. Reversibility: reversible.
- **Guide-name rule (README "Guides named in a review are the guides assigned to that tour")** is checked as a data test over the seed files, and as a post-check only if the heuristic has no false positives on the seed (RESEARCH Pattern 4, rule 7).
</decisions>
